## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a private residential hospitality experience tailored for an elite enclave of 231 homeowners. The aesthetic combines modern architectural precision, quiet luxury, and discreet residential security. The interface mirrors the tangible physical qualities of high-end private villas: polished stone, brushed champagne metals, hand-troweled warm lime washes, and manicured private grounds.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Sovereign & Serene:** Imparts unwavering peace of mind, effortless command of private estate services, and calm sanctuary.
- **Architectural Tactility:** Avoids loud digital gimmicks or generic tech tropes in favor of structural clarity, generous proportions, and material warmth.
- **Discreet Exclusivity:** Interacting with the interface feels like speaking with a world-class private estate manager or private concierge.

### Visual Style
A tailored fusion of **Minimalist Warmth** and **Tactile Luxury**. Crisp hairline metallic borders pair with soft, layered cream containers and deep imperial forest emeralds to anchor security operations, amenity bookings, guest access authorizations, and smart-villa telemetrics.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic architectural materials: deep patinated bronze and forest foliage, brushed champagne gold fixtures, natural travertine, and charcoal slate.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0D3B2E` — Imperial Emerald):** Signifies security, sovereign authority, and core residential identity. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation states, high-priority status indicators, and prominent branding anchors.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` — Champagne Gold):** Serves as a tactile accent for exclusive concierge tiers, guest passes, badge borders, and VIP notifications. A brighter champagne tint (`#D4AF37`) is reserved exclusively for micro-highlights and active ratings.
- **Tertiary (`#2D5A4C` — Cypress Moss):** A mid-tone botanical green providing tonal variation for selected chips, secondary toggles, subtle status pills, and card header accents.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1A2421` — Deep Charcoal Slate):** Grounds the typographical hierarchy with high contrast and legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#4A5550` — Weathered Slate):** Used for secondary copy, metadata, timestamps, and resting state icons.
- **Canvas & Surface System:**
  - Base Screen Background: `#FBF9F5` (Warm Alabaster Sand)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Chalk White)
  - Elevated / Inset Surface: `#F5F1EA` (Pressed Travertine Cream)
  - Hairline Border & Divider: `#E8E2D7` (Raw Silk Grey)
  - Metallic Trim Border: `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.28)`

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances classical residential prestige with clean digital ergonomics. 

- **Display & Headlines (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys timeless refinement, literary authority, and architectural grace. Used selectively for screen titles, welcome headers, villa numbering, and modal announcements.
- **Body & Functional Text (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry, exceptional optical legibility at small sizes, and open counters. Governs all operational metrics, telemetry numbers, input values, and conversational status updates.
- **Labeling & Micro-Typography:** `label-sm` leverages slight tracking (`0.08em`) and uppercase transformation for gate clearances, security status codes (e.g., `APPROVED`, `EN ROUTE`, `ON-PREMISE`), and architectural sub-headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a mobile-first fluid grid optimized for edge-to-edge touch targets on iOS and Android devices, anchoring high-frequency actions within comfortable thumb reach.

### Rhythm & Proportions
- **Canvas Boundaries:** Screen horizontal edge padding uses `margin` (`1.25rem` / `20px`) for standard cards and lists, expanding to `margin-lg` (`1.75rem` / `28px`) on spacious landing dashboards.
- **Grid Structure:** A 4-column fluid layout with `gutter` (`1rem` / `16px`) spacing governs villa feature grids (e.g., HVAC, lighting, clubhouse amenities, guest logs).
- **Component Breathing Room:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-md` (`16px`) for compact listing modules and `space-lg` (`24px`) for hero status placards (e.g., FastPass Gate QR codes, community security alerts).
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Content sections are grouped using `space-xl` (`2.25rem` / `36px`) gaps to preserve uncluttered visual pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on warm, physical layering that reflects architectural materiality.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Base Canvas):** Set to `#FBF9F5`. Elements sitting directly on the canvas are flat or framed with hairline borders.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Sheets):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers lifted above the canvas using a tinted ambient diffusion:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.02);`
  - Paired with a delicate boundary rule: `border: 1px solid #E8E2D7`.
- **Prestige Accents:** Featured cards (e.g., Club Privé booking, Active Gate Pass) introduce a hairline metallic gold boundary:
  - `border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35);`
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px -4px rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08);`
- **Floating Controls & Navigation:** Bottom navigation bar and floating dispatch triggers use a subtle frosted treatment (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.88); border-top: 1px solid #E8E2D7`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances contemporary architectural edges with comfortable ergonomics (`roundedness: 2`).

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` — 1rem / 16px):** Primary form factor for all content units, vehicle entry cards, quick-action tiles, and settings panels.
- **Interactive Triggers & Fields (`rounded` — 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, button components, and search rows for structural confidence.
- **Interactive Badges & Security Pills:** Overridden to full radius (`9999px`) for guest entry status badges, villa occupancy indicators, and operational switches.
- **Sheets & Action Drawers (`rounded-xl` — 1.5rem / 24px):** Applied solely to top corners (`border-top-left-radius` and `border-top-right-radius`) of bottom sheets.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Action (Estate Emerald):** Solid background of `#0D3B2E`, high-contrast text in `#FBF9F5`, font `label-lg`. Height: `52px`. Gentle active scale down (`0.98`) upon press.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Gold Outline):** Border `1.5px solid #C5A059`, background transparent or `#F5F1EA` on hover, text `#0D3B2E`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#0D3B2E` with trailing minimal arrow or icon; zero container background.
- **Emergency / Security Dispatch:** Deep crimson highlight `#8A1C14` with `#FFFFFF` text, isolated with deliberate confirmation friction (swipe-to-dispatch or long press).

### Status Badges & Gate Passes
- **Format:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-sm` uppercase.
- **Access Granted / Pre-Approved:** Soft mint ground (`#E8F2ED`), emerald border (`rgba(13, 59, 46, 0.2)`), emerald text (`#0D3B2E`).
- **Pending / Gate Arrival:** Soft gold ground (`#FAF4E6`), gold border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`), rich amber-gold text (`#8A6B1C`).
- **Restricted / Expired:** Light stone ground (`#F0EDE6`), slate border (`#D5CEC2`), slate text (`#4A5550`).

### Cards & Residence Units
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E2D7`, radius `16px`.
- Card headers feature an architectural sub-title in `label-sm` (`#C5A059`) positioned directly above the card title in `headline-sm` (`#1A2421`).
- Media banners inside cards (e.g., clubhouse pool status, landscape updates) use a fixed `16:9` aspect ratio with seamless top corner clipping.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `52px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5CEC2`, radius `8px`. Active focus shifts border to `#0D3B2E` with a `2px` champagne focus ring (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`). Placeholder copy in `#4A5550` at 60% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Emerald `#0D3B2E` checked state, enclosing a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or champagne dot. Unchecked state uses `#D5CEC2` border over `#FFFFFF`.
- **Toggle Switches:** Track measures `48px x 28px`. Inactive track `#E8E2D7`; active track `#0D3B2E`. Knob is polished porcelain white (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-drop-shadow.

### Tailored Community Modules
- **FastPass Guest QR Card:** Elevated `#FFFFFF` card framed by a 1px metallic champagne stroke. Houses the dynamic QR token, vehicle registration tags in monospaced Manrope, and single-tap share actions via encrypted messaging.
- **Concierge Intercom Row:** Card unit with live audio frequency visualization or direct video call connection to the master guardhouse or estate manager.
- **Villa Telemetry Glance:** A balanced 2x2 grid reporting private irrigation status, HVAC consumption, and parcel delivery alerts using refined line icons and clean numerical readouts.
